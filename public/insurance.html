Key Information

Important conditions

  • International drivers: Elite Cover may cost 1.5 times the standard rate due to higher risk.
  • Exclusions: Damage caused by negligence, intentional actions or breach of the rental agreement is not covered.
  • Compliance: The vehicle must be driven in accordance with all New Zealand road laws.
  • Elite Cover limitations: Terms may vary depending on vehicle type, rental dates and the signed rental agreement.
  • Final decision: Blue Rental reserves the right to determine coverage applicability.
